# Motus 0.19.0 — Retention & Legal Hold v1

Motus 0.19.0 adds the jurisdiction-neutral Retention & Legal Hold v1 boundary
accepted in ADR-040. Immutable producer declarations, exact scope snapshots,
retention applications, trigger occurrences, and bounded custody observations
remain distinct evidence types. Read-only verification reports supplied lineage,
scope, binding, and blocker findings without selecting applicable law, inferring
legal authority or custody, or emitting disposal clearance.

## Verification and cost

Release characterization evidence will be recorded here from three independent
Jenkins relative runs against v0.18.0, the unchanged v0.6.1 cumulative anchor,
one absolute Python 3.10.12 run, and—if the cumulative arm remains red—the fresh
ADR-018 real-workload qualification. No figures are inherited from v0.18.0.

The release workflow will append the SHA-256 of the exact wheel and sdist. Those
bytes will be stored as an immutable workflow artifact and as draft GitHub
Release assets. Publishing that draft would trigger PyPI and is not authorised.
